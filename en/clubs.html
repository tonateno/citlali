Clubs and holidays

Wednesdays and holidays in three languages

The activity centre runs sessions on Wednesdays and during school holidays, exploring languages, cultural diversity, and education in social and emotional skills, citizenship and living together.

Wednesdays

A bilingual, welcoming and active Wednesday

Citlali offers Wednesday care for children enrolled at Citlali and for those from outside the school. Arts and crafts, sports and cooking activities are offered in all three languages.

Wednesday morning

Children are welcomed by French-, English- and Spanish-speaking activity leaders in a warm, nurturing atmosphere. The morning begins with a group gathering and a session of English and Spanish, followed by arts and crafts, sports or cooking.

Wednesday afternoon

After some quiet time, the children come together for an English session, followed by a sports activity suited to their age.

Holidays

Bilingual holidays, a new theme every week

Citlali offers bilingual (English–French) holiday sessions with an introduction to Spanish. The programme of activities follows school-term hours. Open to all.

  • English-, Spanish- and French-speaking activity leaders
  • A different theme each week
  • Open during the shorter school breaks (except Christmas) and in the summer
