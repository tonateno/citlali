Our educational approach

Social and emotional skills: a subject in their own right

On top of the French curriculum, we teach children to feel at ease with themselves, with others and with the world around them. Citlali draws on alternative approaches to education grounded in neuroscience.

  • Small-group teaching, from the first year of nursery to the end of primary
  • Teaching that helps children understand and manage their emotions
  • Experiments, projects and workshops in close contact with nature

Clubs and holidays

Bilingual Wednesdays and holidays, with an introduction to Spanish

The activity centre runs sessions to explore languages, cultural diversity, social and emotional skills and living together. Open to all, with French-, English- and Spanish-speaking activity leaders.

  • Bilingual Wednesdays, morning and afternoon
  • Bilingual holidays, with a different theme each week
  • Open to all children aged 3 to 12
