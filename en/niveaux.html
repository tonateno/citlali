Year groups

From nursery to secondary, in small groups

Citlali welcomes children from Petite Section to CM2, and up to 3ème at secondary level. The French curriculum is enriched by daily language immersion and teaching in social and emotional skills.

Ages 3 to 6

Nursery (maternelle)

Petite, Moyenne and Grande Section: learning together and living together, immersed in three languages.

Petite Section
  • Developing language
  • Discovering written language
  • Learning to be a pupil
  • Moving and expressing yourself through the body
  • Discovering the world
  • Perceiving, imagining, feeling, creating
  • Understanding and managing emotions (Citlali programme)
  • Discovering other cultures and understanding the English used in class (Citlali programme)
  • Discovering Spanish
Moyenne and Grande Section
  • Learning together and living together
  • Using language in all its spoken and written forms
  • Acting, expressing yourself and understanding through the arts
  • Building the first tools for structuring thought
  • Exploring the world
  • Understanding and managing emotions (Citlali programme)
  • Acting, expressing yourself and understanding through physical activity
  • Discovering other cultures, understanding and expressing yourself in English (Citlali programme)
  • Discovering Spanish
From CP to CM2

Primary (élémentaire)

The French national curriculum, enriched by genuine daily language immersion and teaching in social and emotional skills.

What we add to the curriculum
  • Daily language immersion in French, English and Spanish
  • Social and emotional skills: a subject in their own right
  • Eco-citizenship projects
  • Teaching in small groups
Up to 3ème

Secondary (collège)

From 6ème to 3ème, in small classes, in keeping with the school's ethos.
