The school

Nurturing children who are happy, sociable, curious, self-confident and open to the world

Citlali is an international nursery and primary school. It is independent and secular, and is not affiliated to any political or religious movement.

Our mission

To help children enjoy learning more and be happy to go to school, in partnership with their families.

From the very start, we give children the tools to manage their emotions rather than deny them. This lets them make the best use of their intellectual abilities and meet the challenges of today's world.

In time, pupils will be able to make sense of the world around them.

A private school outside the state contract, faithful to the French curriculum

The French academic curriculum is enriched by daily language immersion and teaching in social and emotional skills.

French curriculum

Children follow the French academic curriculum, from Petite Section to CM2, in small groups.

Language immersion

Genuine daily language immersion in French, English and Spanish, woven into classroom life.

Social and emotional skills

A subject in its own right, inspired by alternative approaches to education grounded in neuroscience.

In close contact with nature

Arriving at Citlali each day means rediscovering a world of possibilities, in an atmosphere of joy and enthusiasm for discovery: outdoor experiences, classroom workshops, projects and experiments.
